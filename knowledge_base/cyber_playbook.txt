1. THE CYBER-PHYSICAL ANOMALY FRAMEWORK
This playbook is triggered automatically when the Max-Plus Algebraic (MPA) observer detects a persistent "latency_delta" (Actual Time > Expected Time) that cannot be explained by Calendar Constraints (i.e., is_working = TRUE). Because modern Operational Technology (OT) malware is often stealthy and evades traditional IT firewalls, we use the physical time delay of the machines as the ultimate intrusion signature. 



2. ATTACK PROFILE: DDoS (Distributed Denial of Service) / UDP FLOOD
- SYMPTOMS: Positive latency delta (+ seconds) on Max-Plus predictions. Network monitoring will show high packet collision rates or bandwidth saturation at the edge router.
- PHYSICAL IMPACT: PLCs (Programmable Logic Controllers) fail to receive scheduling tokens on time, causing the physical machine (e.g., P5_Painting) to wait, delaying the schedule.
- MITIGATION DIRECTIVE: 
  a) Physically or logically isolate the OT edge router connected to the affected machine zone.
  b) Switch the local PLC to standalone/offline fallback mode.
  c) Check the Factory SOP for available resource rerouting. 
  d) REROUTING LOGIC: If the backup resource is unavailable (Resource Status = 1), you must halt the upstream process to prevent physical damage. Do not route data through the compromised subnet.

3. ATTACK PROFILE: MITM (Man-in-the-Middle) / DATA INJECTION
- SYMPTOMS: Sudden, isolated command latency on a single robotic node (e.g., res_R1_RobotArm). The network traffic volume may appear entirely normal to IT firewalls, but the MPA delta will show severe deviations as the machine executes malicious, injected sub-routines.
- PHYSICAL IMPACT: Potential destruction of the product or damage to the robot arm due to altered physical coordinates.
- MITIGATION DIRECTIVE:
  a) Instantly disconnect the compromised PLC from the main IT/OT bridge. Do not trust sensor data from this node.
  b) Flush the machine's active job buffer to prevent corrupted manufacturing.
  c) Trigger Safety Interlock Level 2 (Physical power disconnect).
  d) STRICT RULE: Do NOT reroute any jobs to this specific machine until its firmware hash is verified by the security team. Inform the operator that the machine is "Quarantined".

4. ATTACK PROFILE: RANSOMWARE / CASCADE FAILURE
- SYMPTOMS: Latency deltas spiking simultaneously or sequentially across multiple machines (e.g., P1 through P6 showing positive deltas within a 10-second window). 
- PHYSICAL IMPACT: Total loss of production scheduling. Potential lockdown of Human-Machine Interfaces (HMIs).
- MITIGATION DIRECTIVE:
  a) INITIATE TOTAL FACTORY SHUTDOWN. Do not attempt to reroute jobs, as the entire scheduling matrix is compromised.
  b) Sever the physical fiber-optic link between the corporate IT network and the OT factory floor network to prevent lateral movement.
  c) Reboot all local PLC controllers from secure, read-only backup images.

5. OPERATIONAL RECOVERY (POST-INCIDENT)
Once the cyber-threat is neutralized, the system cannot simply be turned back on. The RAG system must recalculate the initial state vector x(0) using the current physical location of all half-finished products, and re-run the Max-Plus baseline matrix A to generate a new production schedule.
